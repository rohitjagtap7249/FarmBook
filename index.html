FarmBook

A simple farm record app for Android

FarmBook helps farmers keep their own records on their phone: sprays, drip applications, labour, expenses, earnings, chemicals, tasks and reminders. It works offline and does not need an account.

Optional online backup

If you choose, FarmBook can save a backup copy of your records in a hidden app folder inside your own Google Drive, so you can restore it on a new phone. Only you can access it.
