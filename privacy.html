FarmBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

What FarmBook stores

FarmBook stores your farm records (crops and plots, sprays, drip applications, labour, expenses, earnings, chemicals, stock, tasks and reminder settings) on your own phone. This data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up.

Google sign-in and Google Drive backup (optional)

If you tap "Sign in with Google" in the Online Backup section, FarmBook asks for one permission: access to its own hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). FarmBook uses it only to save and restore backup files of your farm records. It cannot see or change your other Drive files. Backup files are stored in your own Google account, and the developer cannot see them. FarmBook also reads your Google account email address only to show which account is connected.

What we do not do

FarmBook has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. The developer has no server and no access to your records.

Notifications

Task and reminder notifications are created on your phone from your own records. No data is sent anywhere to produce them.

Your control

You can sign out of Google in the app, delete backups from your Google account settings (Manage apps), or uninstall FarmBook to remove local data.
